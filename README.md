# Andrea Rivas — Portfolio

Welcome to my personal portfolio. This website presents my background as a student, the skills I am developing, and selected projects from my learning journey in technology and digital creation.

## About

I am a student from El Salvador interested in technology, programming, creativity, and building digital experiences that can make a positive impact.

## Portfolio Sections

- **About Me:** My interests, learning journey, and goals.
- **Skills:** Technical and personal skills.
- **Certifications & Achievements:** Courses, certifications, and learning experiences.
- **Projects:** A selection of academic and personal projects.
- **Contact:** Ways to get in touch.

## Featured Projects

### ParkingSV

A digital platform concept that helps people discover parking options in El Salvador and connects drivers with parking-space providers.

[Explore ParkingSV](https://parkingsv.vercel.app/login?redirect=%2Fparqueos)

### Primer Paso

A platform concept that supports young people and first-time job seekers with job-search tools, CV creation guidance, interview preparation, and career resources.

[View the live project](https://andreasrivas.github.io/Primer-Paso/)

### Asistente Visual Rápido

An accessibility-focused prototype exploring camera-based obstacle recognition and audio feedback for people with visual impairments.

[View the live project](https://andreasrivas.github.io/Asistente-para-personas-no-videntes/)
